About the data and scoring

The trained ally and counter-pick coefficients use the project’s 2017-era Kaggle round sample. Ubisoft’s latest published operator win-delta charts are for Y11S2.3, included with the Y11S3 notes, while the current patch is Y11S3.1. Those charts are image-only and don’t provide round compositions, so they cannot refresh interactions. Modern operators without coefficients are shown as unmodeled and are excluded from ranking; no current-game performance is invented.
